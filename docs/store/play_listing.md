The free, ad-free NEET & JEE practice app — 18,000+ verified previous year questions, with solutions, fully offline.

Question X is a focused PYQ practice engine for NEET, JEE Main, and JEE Advanced aspirants. No ads. No paywalls. No login. Just clean, verified questions and solutions you can practice offline.

🎯 What's inside (NEET 2026, JEE 2026 ready)

• JEE Main — every shift 2019-2026, all sessions, ~15,000 questions
• JEE Advanced — Paper 1 + Paper 2, 2013-2026
• NEET / AIPMT — 2005-2026, all sessions
• Every question has a worked solution
• Every chemical formula, equation, and diagram renders crisply with our LaTeX engine
• 4,000+ diagrams, watermark-free, delivered on first sync

🛠 Built for serious aspirants

• Custom Mock Generator — filter by exam, year, subject, topic. Build a "Last 5 years JEE Main Mathematics Calculus" test in seconds.
• Year-range presets — All / Last 5 / Last 10, because 24 years of papers shouldn't need 24 taps.
• Live question count — see how many questions match your filter before you start.
• Mistake Notebook — every wrong answer auto-saved for spaced revision; swipe to mark mastered.
• Practice history — full timeline of every session with score, duration, and a one-tap Review.
• PDF export — generate a print-ready mock test from any filter combination.

📱 Why students choose Question X

• 100% offline after the first sync — practice on the bus, in the metro, anywhere
• Dark-mode-first UI designed for marathon study sessions
• No ads, no paywalls, no account or personal data required
• Open feedback loop — tap the flag icon on any question to report an error; we patch it in the next update

For NEET (UG) and JEE (Main & Advanced) aspirants. Class 11 & 12 Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology revision.
